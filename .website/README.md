RGBeat
Give the colour back.

Controls: Arrows to move and jump. Q / W / E for combos. R for your ultimate once it's charged every colour, all at once.

The city lost every colour. You're the last unicorn left carrying any — and you're getting it back the hard way: dashing, striking and sweeping through a world stripped down to ink and grey, one colour at a time.

Read the colour. Answer it.

Every attack is a two-key combo. The first key picks how you strike dash through with Q, punch ahead with W, sweep wide with E. The second names the colour. Match an enemy's own colour exactly and it shatters in one hit; land the wrong one and you only chip away at it.
Press the same key twice for a pure colour. Press two different keys for a mixed one orange, purple, cyan pulled straight from what red, green and blue make together.
Four levels, four bosses. Each carries every colour at once, cycling which one is exposed telegraphed just long enough to react, tighter with every level and every hit you land.
Every kill drips a little colour back into the world. Every boss you bring down unleashes a wave of it. Beat the last one and the whole city gets its colour and its noise back.
Full colourblind support from the title screen: three alternate palettes, and every hit-colour has its own shape, so you're never reading hue alone.

A whole broken city in 13KB: procedural unicorn, procedural skyline, synthesised music, zero external assets or accounts.Readable source, build scripts and design docs on GitHub: github.com/milenialdev/RGBeat
